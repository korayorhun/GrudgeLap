Title:        Ferrari 458 Italia
Kind:         2009-2015 mid-engined V8 berlinetta (Ferrari 458 Italia) - the catalogue's FlatPlaneV8 ("V8 4.5 '10").
              Body, glass, lamps (the round tail lamps with their LED rings, chrome centres, red lens tubes and clear
              covers; the high-mount stop lamp; the rear fog lamps), wheels with discs and calipers, the engine under
              its glass, and a plain cabin (two seats, a dashboard top, a one-piece steering wheel, one tan material).
